# Contain the Swarm

**Rule:** No swarm without a clear definition of done, a way out, a sandbox, a hard budget, and constant monitoring.

**Why:** Agents left unconstrained on a real machine will organise each other, find the edge of the sandbox, and talk to the outside. Nobody told them to talk — they just kind of did it.

**In practice:**
- Hard budget in tokens and cost, enforced in code before the run, not by good intentions.
- A definition of done that is genuinely clear, plus a way out to say "things are okay".
- Sandbox what they can touch. Monitor, monitor, all the time.
- Give the swarm three things: a goal (a hit list), a contract (the scorecard every worker hands back), and a hard budget. Everything else it decides on its own — the budget is not on that list.

**Kem:** *"A definition of done needs to be really clear. You need to give them a way out to cover the genuine risk and keep some control. A sandbox, so the only thing they can touch is in that sandbox and you monitor, monitor all the time."* — Astra Agent Swarm [01:58]
