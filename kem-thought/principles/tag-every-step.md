# Tag Every Step D, P or H

**Rule:** Before you build, map the work into steps and mark each one deterministic, probabilistic, or human-in-the-loop.

**Why:** Most skills mix all three without telling you which step is which, so when it breaks you have no idea where to look.

**In practice:**
- Ask the model to do the mapping first — it is cheap and it finds the surprise steps.
- Anything you did not tag, you do not understand.
- A skill is a mixture of text the model follows and scripts the skill runs. Know which is which, per step.
